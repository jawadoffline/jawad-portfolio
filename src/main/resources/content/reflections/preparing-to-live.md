Don't spend your whole life preparing to live.

There is a strange habit many of us develop as we grow older. We keep pushing the good parts of life into the future.

I will travel when I have more money. I will spend more time with my family when work settles down. I will take care of my health once things become less stressful. I will finally relax when I have achieved what I am working towards. And the list keeps growing.

The problem is that life rarely reaches a point where everything is settled. One responsibility ends and another begins. You get the job you wanted, and suddenly there's another position to aim for. You reach a financial goal, and before you can enjoy it, you have already set a bigger one. There is nothing wrong with wanting more. Ambition is important, and working towards a secure future is a responsibility, especially when other people depend on you.

But somewhere along the way, we start treating the present as nothing more than a waiting room for a better life. We forget that the ordinary days we are trying to get through are also our lives.

Having dinner with your family, sitting with your parents without constantly checking your phone, meeting an old friend, taking a short trip, spending an evening doing absolutely nothing productive. These things may not look like achievements, but that doesn't make them any less important.

And there is something else worth remembering. The people around us are growing older while we are busy making plans. Our parents are not getting any younger. Children grow up. Friendships change. Some opportunities to spend time together simply don't come back. Of course, we cannot ignore our responsibilities and start living as though tomorrow doesn't matter. The future requires planning, hard work, and sacrifice. But the present deserves some attention too.

You don't have to achieve everything before allowing yourself to enjoy what you already have.

Keep working for a better tomorrow.\
Just don't forget that today was once a tomorrow you were looking forward to.
