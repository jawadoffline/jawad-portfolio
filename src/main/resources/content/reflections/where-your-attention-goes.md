One thing I have realized is that not everything deserves a piece of your mind.

We live in a time where there is always something to be angry about.

A new controversy, a political statement, another argument online, another piece of bad news, another reason to feel that everything is going in the wrong direction.

And before you know it, you have spent hours thinking about people who do not know you exist, arguing about things you cannot change today, while the things you actually can change are waiting quietly in the background.

Your work, your health, your relationships, the book you wanted to read, the skill you wanted to learn, the conversation you have been postponing with someone you love.

Being informed matters. Looking away from what is happening around us is not the answer. But there is a difference between being informed and being consumed. You do not have to carry the weight of every headline throughout your day. You do not have to have an opinion on everything. And you certainly do not have to allow every piece of information to decide the mood of your entire life.

Attention is limited, wherever you keep placing it, your life slowly follows.

So choose carefully! give some attention to the world, but save enough of it for your own life too. There are things worth knowing, and there are things worth building.

Do not become so busy watching the world fall apart that you forget to build the small part of it that belongs to you!
