A little about Wheelogz

The person behind
the handlebars.

I’m Jawad. A software engineer, a motorcycle rider, and someone who has recorded plenty of rides without ever keeping them in order.

Wheelogz is my way of changing that. It begins with a Lifan KPT 250, after years of riding a Yamaha YBR 125G and a Suzuki GS150, and a wish to keep a proper record of the journeys.

Some entries will be about the motorcycle. Some will be about a place, an ordinary errand, or something that happened along the way. The videos live on YouTube. These are the written stories.
